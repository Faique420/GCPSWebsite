About Us

Independent expertise.
Shared quality goals.

Meet Global Clothing Product Services: a third-party inspection, quality assurance, and technical consultancy provider for the textile and apparel industry.

Who we are

A closer look at
your product.

GCPS provides independent textile and apparel quality control, inspections, and factory technical audits. Our product scope covers fabric, woven and knit garments, home furnishing, sportswear, leather products, and accessories.

Our technical services include pattern development, marker making, fit evaluation on live models or mannequins, and fabric consumption improvement. These services support product development and manufacturing decisions.

We work with clients serving markets in Europe, the USA, Australia, and Asia.

  • Europe
  • USA
  • Australia
  • Asia

Our mission

Quality and compliance,
throughout production.

Our mission is to provide reliable textile product assessments, support technical compliance, and improve product value through quality and lean management practices.

Our work considers the safety and well-being of consumers and the people involved in production.
