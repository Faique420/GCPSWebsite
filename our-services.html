Our Services

From first sample

Inspection, technical audits, and consultancy services for fabric, garments, home furnishing, sportswear, leather products, and accessories.

01 / INSPECTION

Product inspections

Independent product checks at key stages of production, with findings documented for the client’s review.

IPI

Initial production

Inspection at the initial production stage.

DPI

During production

Product review while manufacturing is in progress.

FRI

Final random inspection

Inspection of finished products before the next shipment decision.

  • Fabric inspection
  • Pre-production meetings and PCR / PPR activity analysis
  • Product and risk assessment techniques
  • Inspection reports with observations, recommendations, and supporting photographs or video
02 / AUDIT

Factory technical audits

Technical evaluation and process improvement support for textile and apparel manufacturing.

  • Factory technical and compliance audits
  • Process-control improvement techniques
  • Production excellence support
  • Quality management system improvement
03 / DEVELOPMENT

Product & technical development

Technical input for patterns, fit, and fabric use, including evaluation against customer comments and fit requirements.

  • Pattern creation and development
  • Pattern adjustment for measurement control
  • Fit evaluation on live models or mannequins
  • Product development support
  • Marker making and efficiency improvement
  • Fabric consumption reduction and fabric-saving techniques
04 / CONSULTANCY

Consultancy, sourcing & research

Support for businesses developing products, reviewing production practices, or connecting with customers and vendors.

  • Quality assurance consultancy and training
  • Customer and vendor sourcing support
  • Research and development
  • Quality and lean management practices
  • Product safety and risk assessment support
05 / TESTING & COMPLIANCE

Laboratory testing coordination & compliance support

Sample collection and coordination with affiliated laboratory testing and certification bodies, according to the needs of the assignment.

  • Sample picking and collection for laboratory testing
  • Coordination for textile and leather product testing
  • Social compliance and environmental certification support through affiliated bodies

The laboratory, testing scope, certification body, and applicable requirements are discussed for each enquiry. Testing and certification decisions belong to the relevant provider.
