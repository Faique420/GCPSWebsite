About GCPS

An independent eye on every stitch.

01 — Who we are

GCPS is a professional, independent third-party services provider with extensive experience in textile and apparel quality control, inspection and technical audits — covering fabric, readymade garments (woven and knits), home furnishing, sportswear, leather products and accessories.

Our capabilities extend to pattern development, marker-making efficiency, and fit evaluation against buyer standards — on a live model or mannequin — while keeping fabric consumption as lean as possible on behalf of our clients, retailers, exporters, importers, buying offices, manufacturers and online trading portals.

02 — Our mission

Our mission is to meet the highest standards in textile product inspection and testing. We are committed to technical compliance, giving clients reliable, accurate assessments — and to enhancing product value through industry best practice, quality and lean management.

GCPS plays a pivotal role in protecting the safety and well-being of consumers, employers and employees alike, at every stage of production.

How An Inspection Runs

From pre-production to final report

  1. 01

    Pre-production meeting

    Align on specs, patterns and MMT control before a single unit is cut.

  2. 02

    Initial Production Inspection — IPI

    Verify raw materials, trims and early-stage output against approved samples.

  3. 03

    During Production Inspection — DPI

    Catch issues mid-run, while there is still time and stock to correct them.

  4. 04

    Final Random Inspection — FRI

    AQL-based random sampling of finished, packed goods before shipment.

  5. 05

    Fabric & compliance audit

    Fabric inspection alongside factory technical and social-compliance audits.

  6. 06

    Lab testing & reporting

    Sample collection for textile and leather testing, with a report delivered promptly — backed by photos and video.

Also included as needed
PCR / PPR activity analysis Pattern adjustment for MMT control Quality management system improvement Fabric-consumption reduction
