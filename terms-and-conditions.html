1. Website purpose

This website introduces the services of Global Clothing Product Services (GCPS). Its content provides general information about inspections, technical audits, quality assurance, and consultancy.

Submitting an enquiry or opening a WhatsApp conversation does not, by itself, confirm a booking or create an agreed service engagement.

The scope of work, inspection criteria, location, schedule, fees, deliverables, and any cancellation or payment terms must be agreed with GCPS for the specific assignment.

Any separate written service agreement governs the services covered by that agreement. This website does not replace agreed buyer specifications, test methods, inspection protocols, or contract terms.
