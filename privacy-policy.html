1. About this policy

This policy describes information associated with the Global Clothing Product Services (GCPS) website and its enquiry options. For privacy questions, contact Muhammad Younas on +92 333 4664500.

2. Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form lets you enter your name, company, email address, selected service, and message. Company and email are optional. Only include information needed for your enquiry; avoid sending passwords, financial account details, or sensitive personal documents.

When you choose “Continue to WhatsApp”, the entered information is included in a message opened in WhatsApp. You decide whether to send that message. GCPS receives the information you choose to send through WhatsApp or provide by phone.

3. Enquiries and communication

Information sent to GCPS is used to understand your request, respond to you, and discuss the services you require. A later service engagement may involve additional information and separate agreed terms.

The website enquiry form has no direct submission database. It does not send an email or submit your details to a GCPS web server in the background.

4. Website technology and hosting

This website’s own code does not set cookies, store form entries in your browser, or include advertising or analytics trackers. Your hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and secure the website.

Third-party services reached or loaded through this website operate separately and may process information under their own policies.

5. WhatsApp and Google Maps

WhatsApp links open an external service. When you use the enquiry form, the message text is passed to WhatsApp in the link that opens. WhatsApp’s handling of information is explained in its Privacy Policy.

The Contact Us page embeds Google Maps. Loading the map connects your browser to Google. Information associated with Google Maps is handled under Google’s Privacy Policy. You may contact GCPS by phone instead of using the map or WhatsApp.

6. Records and privacy requests

Messages sent to GCPS may remain in the communication service and business records used to handle your enquiry. The website does not automatically delete those records. Contact GCPS to ask about retention, access, corrections, deletion, or any concern about your information.

The response to a request depends on the records held and any applicable business or legal obligations.

7. Changes and contact

GCPS may update this page when website features or information practices change. The date above identifies the current version of this page.
